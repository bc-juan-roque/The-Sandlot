# The Sandlot

Shareable, single-file interactive prototypes, published with GitHub Pages from the repo root.
Each prototype is self-contained in its own folder:

```
<project>/
  index.html   ← the published page  →  https://bc-juan-roque.github.io/The-Sandlot/<project>/
  src/         ← anything used to produce it (templates, styles, scripts, assets, build script)
```

| Prototype | Folder |
|---|---|
| Consolidated commerce.com navigation | `commerce-nav/` |

## Adding a prototype

1. Create `<project>/index.html` (keep it self-contained: inline CSS, JS, SVGs). Put working files in `<project>/src/`.
2. Add a card for it in the root `index.html`.
3. Commit and push. Pages redeploys in about a minute.

## commerce-nav

`commerce-nav/index.html` is generated. Edit the files in `commerce-nav/src/`, then run:

```
python3 commerce-nav/src/build.py
```

Open `commerce-nav/index.html` in a browser to review it (Host Grotesk loads from Google Fonts, so be online for the font).

| File in `src/` | What it is |
|---|---|
| `shell.template.html`, `css/shell.css`, `js/shell.js` | Review shell: address bar, version and variation switcher, review notes, live controls |
| `desktop.html` | Desktop prototype: Commerce bar, BigCommerce and Feedonomics Solution bars, every menu, wireframe page |
| `css/styles.css`, `js/nav.js` | Desktop styles (tokens from the Multi-Brand design system, BC mode) and behavior |
| `css/mobile.css`, `js/mobile.js` | Mobile styles and behavior. The mobile page is generated by `build.py` from the menus in `desktop.html`, so links and content match |
| `js/embed.js` | Lets the prototype pages talk to the shell |
| `assets/` | Commerce logo and the eight Feedonomics platform logos, black at rest and full color on hover (each logo has a `-color.svg` twin; the two cross-fade). The Custom icon is Streamline's "Horizontal Slider 4" (Core), padded so it sits at the weight of the other logos; the credit is kept inside the file |
| `build.py` | Inlines everything into one `index.html` |

The gear at the bottom right opens a control panel for animation timing, hover-focus dimming and
placeholder content. "Copy changes" lists what you changed so it can be applied back to the source;
"Reset all" restores the defaults. Defaults live in the `CONTROLS` list in `js/shell.js`.

**Versions and variations.** The bottom-left toolbar has a Version dropdown, Variation buttons (hidden when a version has only one) and a Page switcher. Versions are defined in the `VERSIONS` object in `js/shell.js`; each holds numbered variations.

| Version | Variations | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop | 1–2 | Commerce bar plus a Solution bar for BigCommerce and Feedonomics. Var 1 = menus open and close on click. Var 2 = same, and once a menu is open, hovering another menu button the dropdown glides and resizes to the new menu while the contents fade out and in from the sides |
| Mobile | 1–2 | Var 1 = push down: the Commerce menu expands under its bar and pushes the product bar down, one menu open at a time. Var 2 = floating: the Commerce menu is a card below the product bar, and opening it hides the product menu; the product menu can then expand above the card and push it down |

Deep links: `#desktop-1`, `#mobile-2`, and a page after the variation, for example
`#mobile-2/bigcommerce/pricing`. The address bar and URL follow the page you are on.

**Review mode.** The Review toggle lists the open questions for the current version and variation. They live in the `notes` arrays in `VERSIONS` (`js/shell.js`); add or remove entries there.

**Pages.** Every nav and sub-nav item has its own page, titled with the item's label. Routes are generated from the links in `desktop.html`, so adding a link gives it a page. Items that appear in more than one menu share a page.

**Behavior.**
- Menus open on click; Esc or clicking outside closes them. The page is not dimmed on desktop.
- Desktop menus stay inside the window: they are positioned from layout so they never run past either side, their columns wrap onto further rows in a narrow window, and a menu taller than the space under it scrolls inside itself.
- Desktop variation 2 only: menus open on hover (after a short hover intent) and close about 200ms after the pointer leaves the buttons and the menu. Hovering another menu button switches to it: the dropdown box glides and resizes from the old menu to the new one, while the old contents fade out toward where you came from and the new contents fade in from the side you are heading. A mouse click on an open button keeps it open; touch still taps to open and close. The menu buttons have no active fill (neither for an open menu nor for the current page) and no chevron flip in this variation (hover still shows the fill). The gear has controls for the hover timings, glide duration, slide distance and fade-out.
- On load the Commerce bar's logo, nav items and Contact sales fade in one after another, with the same motion as the Solution bar's contents.
- On mobile the product bar has the same reveal (it grows out of the Commerce bar's line, then its name and caret fade in), and the Commerce bar's logo and menu button fade in on load.
- Arriving on a BigCommerce or Feedonomics page, the Solution bar grows down out of the Commerce bar, its bottom line starting at the Commerce bar's line and travelling down (the page below moves with it), then its label, nav items and buttons fade in one after another. The gear has controls for the extend time, the first delay and the gap between items.
- The Commerce bar is sticky on Commerce pages. On BigCommerce and Feedonomics pages it scrolls away and the Solution bar stays pinned.
- **Contact sales modal** (layout D4 in Figma): the Get started button (the Commerce bar's old Contact sales button) opens a modal with an intro, both products as clickable heads (to their home pages, with the same hover and focus-dim behavior as the dropdowns) with their trial and demo actions on the dropdown's connector lines, and a Contact sales banner in the style of the AI Labs strip. It closes with the X, the dimmed page, Esc or by choosing a link; focus is kept inside it and returned to the button. On mobile it stacks into one column. Source: `js/modal.js`, `.modal*` in `css/styles.css`, markup at the end of `desktop.html`.
- The Get started button appears only on Commerce pages (desktop bar and mobile menu). On BigCommerce and Feedonomics pages the Solution bar carries the calls to action: on desktop the button fades down and out as that bar arrives, and fades back up when you return.
- Menu links go medium weight, nudge 4px right and show an arrow on hover. On a link with a badge (Learning > Agentic Commerce, New) the badge sits close to the text, moves with it, and the arrow comes after the badge. Hovering a menu item dims the others, brings the hovered item up quickly, then the others fade back after a short pause.
- Hovering a product sub-item under Solutions lights its connector: the curve turns black and a black gradient runs up the line from it, fading into the usual line color.
- Cards (Surface, Integrations, the AI Labs strip) are one big link.
- On mobile, rows with a chevron drill into a panel with a Back row, and a scrim dims the page while any menu is open (tap it to close).

Motion timing and easing are variables at the top of `css/styles.css` and `css/mobile.css`; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns animation off.
